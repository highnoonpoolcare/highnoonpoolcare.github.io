North Texas does not ease into winter. We get a stretch of pleasant November days and then a front comes through and drops thirty degrees in an afternoon. The first serious front is the right prompt to get a few things handled.

## Know where your equipment shutoffs are

If you have never located the breaker for your pool equipment, do it now, in daylight, in comfortable weather. Not at ten at night when it is twenty-eight degrees and something is wrong.

Same for any valves you would need to turn. Know them before you need them.

## Check freeze protection, if you have it

Many North Texas pool systems have a freeze guard that runs the pump automatically when temperature drops near freezing. That protection is only useful if it works.

Confirm the sensor is not damaged or sitting somewhere unrepresentative, and that the system is actually set to engage. We see freeze guards that have been quietly disabled for years, usually by someone troubleshooting something unrelated.

## Clear the debris before it gets cold

You want baskets and the pool floor reasonably clear going into winter. Debris breaking down all winter under a cover or in cold water gives you a considerably harder spring.

This is also the point where the bulk of leaf drop is usually finishing up in the newer neighborhoods, though the mature-tree areas around Denton still have weeks left.

## Get the chemistry right before it cools further

Cold water slows chemical reactions, which makes corrections take longer and work less predictably. It is much easier to balance a pool at seventy degrees than at fifty.

Get pH, alkalinity, and hardness into range now, and winter maintenance becomes mostly a matter of holding steady rather than fixing things.

## What you do not need to do

You do not need to close the pool. Pools in North Texas stay open and circulating year round, and a full northern-style winterization creates more problems here than it solves.

You do not need to drain anything below the skimmer. That is northern advice for climates where the water column genuinely freezes solid, which is not us.

The real work is freeze protection on the handful of genuinely hard nights, which is a separate topic and worth its own attention.
